Where it runs

Execution, simulated: sell a big order fast or slow?

Almgren & Chriss (2000): selling quickly pushes the price down (market impact); selling slowly leaves you exposed to price moves for longer. For each level of risk aversion λ there is a best schedule. Parameters are the paper’s own numerical example: 1,000,000 shares at $50 over 5 days, 30% annual volatility (Table 1 of the authors’ 2000 manuscript).

Simulation

Equations (8), (5) and (17) of the manuscript; impact parameters ε = $0.0625/share, η = 2.5×10⁻⁶, γ = 2.5×10⁻⁷, σ = 0.95 $/share/day½. With λ = 10⁻⁶ the paper reports κ ≈ 0.6 per day; this simulation’s value is shown above. Read from the authors’ preprint; the published Journal of Risk version was not read.
