Strategy cards

Each card gives the rule in words, the equations, the papers it comes from, and, most importantly, how our version differs from the paper. Where the data or the text did not let us copy a paper exactly, the card says so.

    How to read the evidence labels
    Published, replicable
    The method is public and can be reproduced on data we can use.
    Published, data restricted
    The method is public, but the paper’s data are licensed, so our data differ from theirs.
    Public description only
    Only what a firm says about itself; never code or parameters.
    Definition
    A benchmark defined by construction; it makes no claim about markets.

    Confirmation levels for each parameter (paper text, secondary source, unverified) are listed on every card and collected on the Sources page.

    What we don’t show

    The trading algorithms that firms such as Jane Street, Citadel Securities, Virtu, Two Sigma or Renaissance Technologies actually run are trade secrets. Their code, parameters and results are not public, so nothing on this site describes or imitates them.

    What is public are broad categories of strategy that academics have written down: trend following, momentum, mean reversion, portfolio optimization, and market making. This site runs published, simplified versions of those categories. Real firms differ in data, speed, execution, risk control and costs, and nothing here says how they perform.
